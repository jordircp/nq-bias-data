The inflation outlook has also been revised materially higher in the updated staff forecasts. This partly reflects higher fuel and other commodity prices affected by the conflict in the Middle East. But domestic capacity pressures are also judged to have been a little tighter than earlier thought. AI-related investment is driving rapid growth in prices for technology-related goods, and inflation in many goods and services remains high. Inflation is now forecast to remain elevated through much of 2027 and is not expected to reach the top of the 2–3 per cent target range until late 2027, around three quarters later than previously forecast. This presents upside risks to inflation expectations; some measures are assessed to have risen lately. The increases in the cash rate target since the beginning of the year have tightened financial conditions. The economy is slowing, housing prices have fallen significantly and the unemployment rate has risen. The forecasts see growth in output slowing further over the coming year, which is expected to reduce capacity pressures and help return inflation to target.
